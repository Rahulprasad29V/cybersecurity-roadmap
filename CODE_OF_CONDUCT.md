# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and constructive learning community.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, patient and inclusive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Explain disagreements professionally.
- Credit original work and respect licenses.
- Share knowledge without shaming beginners.
- Keep discussions focused on learning and responsible security.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, personal attacks, spam, plagiarism, sharing private information, or encouraging illegal activity are not acceptable.

## Reporting
Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact method listed on the GitHub repository profile. Do not publish sensitive evidence or personal information in an issue.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject content that violates this policy.
