# Rubric: Technical Report

| Criterion | Weight | Strong evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Executive summary | 10% | Accurate, concise and audience-appropriate |
| Scope and context | 10% | Assets, authorization, assumptions and exclusions stated |
| Methodology | 15% | Reproducible and proportionate |
| Evidence | 20% | Relevant, sanitized and sufficient for claims |
| Technical analysis | 15% | Root cause and limitations explained |
| Impact and risk | 10% | Business impact and uncertainty reasoned |
| Remediation | 10% | Actionable, prioritized and testable |
| Clarity and traceability | 10% | Consistent terminology, references and evidence links |

## Review questions
- Could another qualified reviewer reproduce the reasoning?
- Does every claim have evidence or a clearly stated assumption?
- Are severity and impact justified rather than asserted?
- Is remediation linked to the root cause?
- Is sensitive information removed?

Safety and authorization are mandatory gates, not weighted bonus criteria.
