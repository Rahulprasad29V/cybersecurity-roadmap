# Rubric: Oral Defense

Use this rubric for a peer review, instructor discussion or self-recorded explanation.

| Criterion | What to assess |
|---|---|
| Conceptual understanding | Can the learner explain core concepts without reading the report verbatim? |
| Evidence reasoning | Can they trace conclusions to evidence? |
| Uncertainty | Can they identify alternative explanations and missing data? |
| Trade-offs | Can they explain why one control or design was selected? |
| Communication | Can they adapt the explanation to the audience? |
| Safety and ethics | Can they explain authorization, privacy and stop conditions? |
| Reflection | Can they identify what they would improve next? |

## Suggested questions
1. Which assumption most affects your conclusion?
2. What evidence would change your mind?
3. What is the most important limitation?
4. What control would you prioritize first and why?
5. How would you verify the remediation?
6. What would make you stop the exercise?
