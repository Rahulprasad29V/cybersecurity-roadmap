# Rubric: Capstone Project

| Criterion | Weight | Advanced performance |
|---|---:|---|
| Problem framing and scope | 10% | Clear objective, authorization, assumptions and boundaries |
| Technical depth | 20% | Correct integration of relevant concepts |
| Method and reproducibility | 15% | Traceable process and repeatable validation |
| Evidence and analysis | 15% | Strong evidence, careful reasoning and explicit uncertainty |
| Security and safety | 15% | Safe-by-design decisions, privacy and cleanup |
| Remediation and impact | 10% | Practical, prioritized and measurable recommendations |
| Communication | 10% | Strong executive summary and technical documentation |
| Reflection | 5% | Honest gaps, trade-offs and future work |

## Completion gate
A capstone must meet the safety criteria, provide usable evidence and explain its limitations. A visually polished deliverable without sound analysis does not pass.
