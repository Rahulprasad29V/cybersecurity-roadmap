# CyberWolf Community Guidelines

CyberWolf learning is collaborative. Use community spaces to ask clear questions, share safe learning artifacts and help other learners grow.

## How to ask a useful question
Include:
- What you are trying to understand.
- What you expected to happen.
- What happened instead.
- The relevant sanitized error or observation.
- What you have already checked.

## Sharing work
- Share only artifacts you own or have permission to publish.
- Remove secrets, tokens, personal data and private target details.
- State whether work was performed in a local lab or approved platform.
- Credit source material, datasets and contributors.
- Explain limitations and avoid overstating results.

## Collaboration expectations
Be respectful, constructive and beginner-friendly. Do not shame learners for mistakes. Follow the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [Contributing Guidelines](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## No unsafe requests
Do not request or share unauthorized target access, credential theft, malware deployment, evasion, persistence, destructive activity or data exfiltration. Reframe questions around defensive analysis, safe labs, detection and remediation.
