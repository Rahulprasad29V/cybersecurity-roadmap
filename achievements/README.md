# CyberWolf Learning Achievements

Achievements are optional motivators for consistent practice and demonstrated learning. They are **self-recorded milestones**, not accredited certifications, formal grades or proof of professional competence.

## How to use
1. Review the criteria in [achievements.json](achievements.json).
2. Save evidence privately or publish a sanitized portfolio artifact.
3. Mark an achievement in your local progress record.
4. Be honest about what you completed and what was independently verified.

## Suggested achievement rules
- Award an achievement only after the listed evidence exists.
- Do not award points for scanning more targets or performing higher-impact actions.
- Prefer quality, reflection, remediation and responsible practice over volume.
- Do not publish personal data, client data or sensitive lab evidence.
