# Day 003 — Set up a learning journal

## Objective
Create folders for notes, lab reports and safe project evidence.

## Today's activity
Create your journal from templates/learning-journal.md and add your first entry.

## Evidence to save
- [ ] Notes in your learning journal
- [ ] Safe evidence: diagram, sanitized screenshot, code or short report
- [ ] One question to research further

## Reflection
1. What did I learn?
2. What was difficult?
3. How did I verify my understanding?
4. What should I revisit?

## Safety reminder
Work only with your own systems, local lab assets, synthetic data or targets explicitly authorized by the owner. Stop if you leave the defined scope.
