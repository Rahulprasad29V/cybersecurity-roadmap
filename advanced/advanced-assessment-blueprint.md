# Advanced Assessment Blueprint

This blueprint helps instructors and self-study learners design balanced assessments. It is not an accredited exam specification.

## Competency domains

| Domain | Suggested weighting | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Security architecture and threat modeling | 15% | Trust-boundary diagram and threat register |
| Detection engineering and threat hunting | 20% | Detection logic, test matrix and hunt report |
| Cloud-native and identity security | 15% | IAM and cloud posture assessment |
| Secure SDLC and supply chain | 15% | CI/CD control matrix and threat model |
| Vulnerability management | 10% | Risk-ranked backlog and retest plan |
| Incident command and resilience | 15% | Tabletop action plan and after-action report |
| Communication and professional judgment | 10% | Executive summary and oral defense |

## Assessment balance
- 20% knowledge and concept explanation.
- 30% scenario analysis and decision-making.
- 35% practical artifact and evidence quality.
- 15% communication, limitations and reflection.

## Required safeguards
- Use only synthetic datasets, local labs or explicitly authorized platforms.
- Do not use live third-party targets as assessment material.
- Do not grade learners on intrusive activity, payload effectiveness or unauthorized access.
- Treat privacy, scope and evidence handling as mandatory pass/fail gates.
- Provide accommodations and accessible assessment formats.

## Evidence standards
A learner should be able to explain what they did, why they did it, what evidence supports the result, what remains uncertain and how the recommendation can be verified.
