# Advanced Module: Incident Command and Resilience

**Level:** Advanced · **Suggested effort:** 6–10 hours

## Outcomes
- Establish incident roles, decision rights and communication rhythms.
- Assess operational impact and choose proportionate containment options.
- Coordinate evidence preservation, legal/privacy escalation and recovery.
- Convert lessons learned into measurable control improvements.

## Core topics
1. Incident severity, activation criteria and command structure.
2. Roles: incident lead, technical lead, communications, legal/privacy and business owner.
3. Situation reports, decision logs and evidence handling.
4. Containment trade-offs and service continuity.
5. Recovery objectives, backups and restoration validation.
6. Post-incident review and tracked corrective actions.

## Practical exercise
Run the [incident response capstone](../capstones/incident-response.md) as a tabletop. No malware execution or live system disruption is required.

## Deliverable
- Incident action plan.
- Situation report and decision log.
- Stakeholder communication plan.
- Recovery checklist.
- Lessons-learned register with owners and due dates.
