# Advanced Module: Security Architecture and Threat Modeling

**Level:** Advanced · **Suggested effort:** 6–10 hours

## Outcomes
- Identify assets, identities, trust boundaries and data flows.
- Model threats and abuse cases using a consistent method.
- Evaluate preventive, detective and recovery controls.
- Explain residual risk and security trade-offs to technical and business stakeholders.

## Core topics
1. Context diagrams, data-flow diagrams and trust boundaries.
2. STRIDE as one threat-identification aid; it is not a complete risk model.
3. Abuse cases, misuse cases and security requirements.
4. Identity boundaries, service-to-service trust and secrets handling.
5. Secure architecture patterns, segmentation and defense in depth.
6. Risk treatment, residual risk, assumptions and decision records.

## Practical exercise
Use the fictional training architecture in [the architecture capstone](../capstones/architecture-review.md). Identify crown-jewel assets, trust boundaries, abuse cases and design controls. Do not model a real system without its owner's approval.

## Deliverable
- Context and data-flow diagram.
- Asset and trust-boundary inventory.
- Threat register with assumptions and evidence.
- Control mapping and residual-risk decision.
- Architecture decision record.
