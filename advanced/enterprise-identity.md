# Advanced Module: Enterprise Identity Security

**Level:** Advanced · **Suggested effort:** 6–10 hours

## Outcomes
- Model identity lifecycle and privilege boundaries.
- Evaluate federation, single sign-on and authentication assurance.
- Review privileged access, service identities and access certification.
- Design controls for account recovery and session revocation.

## Core topics
1. Identity governance: joiner, mover, leaver processes.
2. Authentication assurance, MFA and phishing-resistant authenticators.
3. Federation and trust relationships.
4. Role-based and attribute-based authorization.
5. Privileged access management and break-glass accounts.
6. Service principals, workload identity and non-human identities.
7. Access reviews, audit evidence and exception handling.

## Practical exercise
Use the synthetic [identity access matrix](../datasets/identity-access-matrix.csv). Find role conflicts, orphaned access and separation-of-duty concerns. Propose a least-privilege design and an access-review cadence.

## Deliverable
- Identity lifecycle diagram.
- Role and entitlement matrix.
- Privileged-access control review.
- Access review and exception process.
