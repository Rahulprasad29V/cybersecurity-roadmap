# CyberWolf Advanced 30-Day Engineering Challenge

This challenge is for learners who have completed the foundational 100-day path or can already demonstrate equivalent skills. It emphasizes design, defensible reasoning and portfolio evidence.

## Rules
- Use synthetic datasets, local labs or explicitly authorized environments only.
- Do not test production or third-party systems without written scope.
- Keep personal and organizational data out of public evidence.
- Each week ends with a reviewable artifact.

## Days 1–5: Architecture and risk
1. Select a fictional system and define its business purpose.
2. Inventory assets, identities and sensitive data.
3. Draw context and data-flow diagrams.
4. Identify trust boundaries and abuse cases.
5. Produce a threat register and residual-risk summary.

**Milestone:** Architecture threat model.

## Days 6–10: Detection engineering
6. Choose one defensive detection hypothesis.
7. Identify telemetry sources, fields, time quality and gaps.
8. Write vendor-neutral analytic logic.
9. Create synthetic positive and benign test cases.
10. Evaluate false positives, coverage and triage steps.

**Milestone:** Detection specification and test matrix.

## Days 11–15: Cloud and identity
11. Review the synthetic cloud posture dataset.
12. Prioritize IAM and storage findings.
13. Review service identity and privilege boundaries.
14. Design least-privilege remediation and validation.
15. Write a cloud and identity review.

**Milestone:** Cloud posture report.

## Days 16–20: Secure delivery and vulnerability management
16. Map a demo software delivery pipeline.
17. Threat-model source, build, dependency and release trust.
18. Design secret and dependency controls.
19. Prioritize the synthetic vulnerability backlog.
20. Create a remediation and exception workflow.

**Milestone:** DevSecOps control plan.

## Days 21–25: Incident command and resilience
21. Define incident roles and decision authority.
22. Build a timeline from synthetic event records.
23. Write a situation report with knowns and unknowns.
24. Evaluate containment and recovery trade-offs in a tabletop.
25. Produce an after-action report with owners and due dates.

**Milestone:** Incident response package.

## Days 26–30: Capstone and review
26. Select a capstone and confirm its acceptance criteria.
27. Complete the technical deliverables.
28. Review evidence, privacy and limitations.
29. Present the result using the oral-defense rubric.
30. Publish a sanitized portfolio summary and write a next-quarter development plan.

**Final output:** One reviewed capstone package and a concise learning retrospective.
