# Learning Path: Governance, Risk & Compliance Analyst

**Goal:** Translate business objectives and obligations into practical, measurable security governance.

## Prerequisites
Security fundamentals, written communication, basic IT and organizational process awareness.

## Learning sequence
1. Governance structures, policies, standards and procedures.
2. Asset context, risk identification, likelihood and impact.
3. Risk treatment, owners, exceptions and review dates.
4. Control design, mapping and evidence collection.
5. Third-party risk, privacy, audit readiness and business continuity.
6. Framework awareness: NIST CSF, ISO/IEC 27001 and CIS Controls.

## Practice
Create a risk register for a fictional organization. Map selected risks to controls and define evidence that would demonstrate operation.

## Portfolio evidence
Risk register, policy outline, control matrix and audit evidence plan.

## Readiness checkpoint
Explain the business risk, control owner, evidence quality, residual risk and decision authority. Compliance is not a guarantee of security.
