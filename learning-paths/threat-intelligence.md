# Learning Path: Threat Intelligence Analyst

**Goal:** Turn trustworthy threat information into timely, contextual and defensible analysis.

## Prerequisites
Networking, operating systems, logs, security fundamentals and research writing.

## Learning sequence
1. Intelligence requirements, collection, processing, analysis and dissemination.
2. Indicators, observables, tactics, techniques and procedures.
3. MITRE ATT&CK as a knowledge base, not a complete attribution system.
4. Source evaluation, confidence, context, expiry and false associations.
5. Analytic writing, competing hypotheses and defensive recommendations.
6. Sharing restrictions, privacy and handling of sensitive intelligence.

## Practice
Analyze public, reputable reports and synthetic indicators. Create a short intelligence note that records source, date, confidence, relevance and recommended defensive action.

## Portfolio evidence
An intelligence brief, indicator lifecycle policy and mapping exercise.

## Readiness checkpoint
Explain what the evidence supports, what it does not support and why an indicator may be stale, shared or misleading.
