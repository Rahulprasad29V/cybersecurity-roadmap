# Learning Path: Digital Forensics & Incident Response

**Goal:** Preserve and analyze evidence, construct timelines and support incident handling.

## Prerequisites
Operating systems, filesystems, networking, logs and basic scripting.

## Learning sequence
1. Incident response lifecycle and evidence preservation.
2. Hashes, timestamps, time zones and chain of custody.
3. Windows, Linux, browser and network artifact concepts.
4. Timeline creation and correlation of multiple evidence sources.
5. Investigation hypotheses, confidence and alternative explanations.
6. Containment, recovery, reporting and lessons learned.

## Practice
Use synthetic event sets and purpose-built forensic training images. Preserve originals, record hashes and document every handling step.

## Portfolio evidence
Evidence log, event timeline, investigative notes and final report with limitations.

## Readiness checkpoint
Separate observed facts from interpretations, explain evidence integrity and state which questions remain unanswered.
