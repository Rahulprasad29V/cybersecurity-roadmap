# Lab: Packet Review

**Level:** Beginner · **Effort:** 45–60 minutes

## Objective
Identify basic protocol metadata and explain a simple communication flow using a benign capture.

## Prerequisites
Basic networking and familiarity with Wireshark or another packet viewer.

## Authorized environment
Use a capture generated by your own device or a public training dataset explicitly provided for analysis. Do not capture other people's traffic or inspect a network without permission.

## Activity
1. Open the approved capture in Wireshark.
2. Identify a DNS query and its response, if present.
3. Find a TCP connection and observe source/destination addresses and ports.
4. Identify whether TLS is present; do not assume encrypted payloads can be read.
5. Note what the capture cannot prove, such as user identity or intent.

## Evidence
- A simple sequence diagram.
- A short explanation of one DNS exchange.
- A sanitized note with protocol, time and packet references.

## Success criteria
Correctly distinguish DNS, TCP and TLS metadata and document at least two limitations.

## Reflection
Why does encrypted application payload not make all network metadata invisible?
