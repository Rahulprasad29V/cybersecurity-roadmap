# Curriculum Release and Review Process

## Content lifecycle
1. **Proposed:** a contributor opens an issue or pull request with scope and outcomes.
2. **Draft:** content follows the repository templates and includes authoritative references.
3. **Technical review:** a reviewer checks accuracy, current versions and reproducibility.
4. **Safety review:** a reviewer checks authorization boundaries, data handling and misuse risk.
5. **Editorial review:** a reviewer checks clarity, accessibility and link integrity.
6. **Published:** the change is merged and recorded in the changelog.
7. **Periodic review:** fast-changing topics are reviewed more frequently than stable fundamentals.

## Suggested review cadence
| Content type | Suggested review |
|---|---|
| Vendor tools, cloud services and certifications | Every 6 months or after material changes |
| Vulnerability advisories and threat reports | At each update; check publication dates |
| Standards and frameworks | At least annually or on new edition |
| Core networking and security fundamentals | Every 12–18 months |
| Static templates and rubrics | Annually |

These are suggested maintenance targets, not guarantees that content remains current between reviews.

## Versioning
- **Major:** breaking change to the curriculum manifest or a substantial restructuring of learning outcomes.
- **Minor:** new modules, tracks, datasets or assessment packs.
- **Patch:** factual corrections, clearer explanations and link updates.

## Review record
For important curriculum changes, record reviewer, date, sources checked, safety notes and known limitations in the pull request.
