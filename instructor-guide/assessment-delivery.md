# Instructor Guide: Assessment Delivery

## Before the assessment
- Publish outcomes, rubric, allowed resources and evidence expectations.
- Provide synthetic data and confirm that no live target access is required.
- Explain privacy, collaboration, attribution and academic-integrity rules.
- Offer accessible alternatives for tool-dependent activities.

## During the assessment
- Answer scope and safety questions before activity begins.
- Do not encourage learners to exceed a lab boundary to obtain a stronger result.
- Require learners to distinguish facts, hypotheses and unknowns.
- Record accommodation and clarification decisions consistently.

## Reviewing submissions
1. Confirm the learner used an authorized or synthetic environment.
2. Review evidence integrity and data minimization.
3. Score each rubric criterion with a short rationale.
4. Identify one strength and one specific improvement.
5. Require remediation if a safety gate was violated.

## Feedback pattern
- **Observed:** what the learner demonstrated.
- **Evidence:** where it appears in the submission.
- **Gap:** what remains incomplete or uncertain.
- **Next action:** a measurable improvement.

## Academic integrity
Learners should credit sources, tools, datasets, collaborators and AI assistance where required by the applicable institution or program policy. A polished report is not a substitute for understanding; use an oral explanation or reflective note to confirm reasoning.
