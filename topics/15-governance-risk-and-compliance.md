# Governance, Risk and Compliance

Connect technical controls to business risk and assurance.

## Core concepts
- Security governance, policies, standards, procedures and baselines.
- Risk assessment, risk treatment, risk registers and control ownership.
- Audit evidence, compliance mapping, third-party risk and privacy.
- Business continuity, disaster recovery and security awareness.
- Framework awareness: NIST CSF, ISO/IEC 27001, CIS Controls and OWASP.

## Practice
Create a small risk register for a fictional organization. Map three risks to preventive, detective and corrective controls. Draft a one-page acceptable-use policy outline.

## Check your understanding
- Why is compliance not identical to security?
- Who should own a risk treatment decision?
- What makes audit evidence useful?
