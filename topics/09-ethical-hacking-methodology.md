# Ethical Hacking Methodology

Learn a disciplined, authorized assessment workflow.

## Core concepts
- Written authorization, scope, exclusions, rules of engagement and stop conditions.
- Reconnaissance and asset discovery in permitted environments.
- Service identification, vulnerability assessment and safe validation.
- Evidence handling, risk rating, remediation, retesting and reporting.
- Responsible disclosure and communication with system owners.

## Practice
Use a local intentionally vulnerable VM or platform-provided target. Record scope and methodology before testing. Validate a low-impact finding and document reproducible evidence.

## Safety boundary
Do not scan public IP ranges, campus networks, employer systems or third-party websites unless you have explicit written authorization and a defined scope. Do not perform denial-of-service, destructive, stealth, persistence, credential theft or data-exfiltration activities.

## Check your understanding
- What should happen if testing reaches an out-of-scope asset?
- Why is scanner output not automatically a confirmed vulnerability?
- What belongs in an executive summary?
