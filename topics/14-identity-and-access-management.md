# Identity and Access Management

Protect identities, accounts, privileges and authentication flows.

## Core concepts
- Identity lifecycle, provisioning, deprovisioning and access reviews.
- Authentication factors, MFA, federation, SSO and session lifecycle.
- Role-based and attribute-based access control.
- Privileged access management and service identities.
- Password policy, recovery workflows and phishing-resistant authentication.

## Practice
Design a role matrix for a fictional learning platform. Review a sample access request and justify least privilege. Threat-model account recovery and session revocation.

- How do authentication and authorization differ?
- Why should access be removed promptly when roles change?
- What risks can service accounts introduce?
