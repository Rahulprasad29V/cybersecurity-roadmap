# Digital Forensics and Incident Response

Learn to preserve evidence and investigate incidents systematically.

## Core concepts
- Incident classification, triage, containment, eradication and recovery.
- Evidence integrity, chain of custody, timestamps and time zones.
- Disk, memory, endpoint, network and cloud evidence concepts.
- Timelines, indicators, artifacts and investigative hypotheses.
- Legal, privacy and organizational constraints.

## Practice
Build a timeline from synthetic events. Calculate hashes for sample evidence files and record them. Write an incident report that separates facts from interpretation. Conduct a tabletop exercise.

## Check your understanding
- Why document evidence handling?
- Why preserve original evidence?
- How can clock drift affect an investigation?
